# Nabat TerraPod Challenge

- **Page:** https://challengeon.atrc.ae/en/challenges/ntpc
- **Organiser:** ATRC through ASPIRE, with Nabat AI Technologies (Masdar City). $50,000 prize and a possible commercial partnership with Nabat.
- **Eligibility:** 18 or over; individuals, students, startups, researchers all welcome.
- **Deadline:** Phase 1 proposal by **Thu 19 Nov 2026** (no time zone given; aim for the day before).
- **Task:** a cheap, biodegradable seed carrier that a drone can drop to grow plants in the UAE desert.
  - 2.5 to 3 cm across, survives a 12 to 15 m drop, at most AED 0.40 each, no microplastics.
  - Fits the Nabat drone.
  - Carries seeds from two mixes of three native species:
    - Mix A: *Eremobium aegyptiacum*, *Cyperus conglomeratus*, *Stipagrostis plumosa*
    - Mix B: *Panicum turgidum*, *Calligonum comosum*, *Haloxylon salicornicum*
- **Submit:** participation form + an **8 to 10 slide PDF deck (max 10 MB)**, in English, covering:
  team and experience; carrier make-up (seeds per species), size, how it's made, drone fit;
  past deployment evidence with references; germination data vs nearby reference sites;
  cost (AED 0.40 or less); sustainability and biodegradability; 5-year production capacity;
  readiness (prototype or a credible plan). Disclose any open-source use.
- **Phase 1 judged on:** quality and sustainability, germination, cost, drone fit, production/scale.
- **Later:** shortlist Nov; video pitch Dec; finalists send carriers by 1 Jan 2027; desert test Jan to Feb 2027 (8 to 10 weeks); review Mar; winner Q2 2027.
- **Note:** this is a materials/biology challenge, not software. The deck asks for germination data and past deployments, so real tests (or a partner with them) matter.
